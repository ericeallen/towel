# Proposal review, October 8, 2026

[Proposal index](README.md) · [Documentation index](../README.md)

Most implementation sequences in this directory have shipped. Retain precise
capability recovery and measured exact optimization as active directions; defer
the substantial new designs until their safety obligations and present opportunity
are measured. Keep Template Method research for readable visitor code. Retire
decorator trust overrides, redirection to user-defined functions and assumptions
that weaken ordinary semantic preservation. Preserve dated validation reports as
an archive, not a feature backlog.

This review covers all **15 Markdown files originally present**, including the
index, and the individual ideas inside the larger roadmaps. It was made on
`fix/1792-audit-followup`, using the released source
`44d779563578a32e24f8213fba96f43c6698ab43`, current implementations and focused
live construction probes. Other audit repairs are concurrent work; this review

## Evidence and decision rules

The newest source-bound release campaign is
`~/Knowledge/handoffs/towel/20261007T055833Z-codex-observer-frozen-release-validation/`.
Its `release-complete.json` and appended `HANDOFF.md` record publication of 1.792
from `44d7795`. Reports here retain their older source identities. Their pending
gates and intermediate percentages are not present obligations or current results.

The latest complete production-CLI comparison in that campaign used default
typing, formatting and automatic workers on Packaging 26.3, with eight samples in
PCCPPCCP order. Published 1.772 averaged 39.3557 seconds; 1.792 averaged 39.7143
seconds, 0.911% higher, within approximately 2.8% recorded variation. It establishes
no meaningful whole-command speedup or slowdown on that workload, and no
corpus-wide gain. The older Sphinx first-reveal improvement is a phase measurement,
not a timing of its complete fixed point.

The recommendations apply these rules:

- Preserve ordinary binding, control flow, evaluation order, effects, exceptions,
  interfaces, type contracts and observable object lifetimes. Capability work needs
  a positive extraction fixture and independent before/after execution; removing a
  decline is insufficient.
- Honor the [October 2 reflection decision](../DECISIONS.md#2026-10-02-reflection-and-self-instrumentation-are-outside-the-preservation-contract).
  Do not restore scans or decorator allowlists for excluded observations. Actual
  import effects, callbacks, `global`/`nonlocal` rebinding and dispatch remain
  obligations. Explicit `# towel: no-extract` is the available user control.
- An exact optimization preserves discovered and accepted refactorings, not just a
  count or a selected golden. Compare candidate identities, materialization and
  final accepted output with the optimization disabled. Do not skip pairs, loosen
  validation, lower budgets or replace exhaustive discovery with an incomplete
  search to produce a speed claim.
- Cache structural facts by structure only when that is their complete input.
  Safety judgments depend on module, enclosing function, position and bindings;
  `BlockSite` and `_per_block` in
  [`block_analysis.py`](../../src/towel/unification/block_analysis.py) make this
  distinction explicit. A proof for one occurrence is not reusable solely because
  another occurrence has the same statements. Bound cache lifetime and capacity,
  keep values immutable, and test eviction, invalidation and disabled-cache parity.
- Measure complete cold-to-final production paths on real inputs, including
  checkers, formatting and final cold confirmation. Count repeated inputs before
  proposing a cache; use an algorithmic change when requests are distinct.

Priorities are recommendations, not authorization or commitments: **P1** is the
next bounded investigation; **P2** is retained work behind proof and opportunity
gates; **P3** is research or presentation work after the more consequential work.
Completed and retired ideas have no implementation priority.

## Dethunking: four small items and one unsafe assumption

1. **Evaluation-order parameter ordering: retain P2.** `inline_leading_thunks`
   still stops when a second thunk would violate argument order. A reversed-order
   live fixture inlines only `__param_1`. Reordering the private signature must
   remap all calls, substitution, type rows, inventories and reuse; test every
   argument's effects, availability and lifetime, not just the thunks.
2. **Plain-name callees: revisit only fresh refusals, P2.** Current construction
   already passes/inlines eligible callees and rejects residual variadic forwarding
   lambdas. Do not restore a wrapper or snapshot a late-rebound callee merely to
   remove a historical census row. Prove the exact lookup timing of a current case.
3. **Pure/impure sites sharing one thunk parameter: defer P3.** Converting only
   one site requires a new representation or specialization; it can add helpers
   instead of improving readability. No present payoff is established.
4. **Bare-name thunks: retain definite-assignment precision, P2.** Current
   `available_argument_names` covers module/enclosing binding, deletion and nearer
   shadowing. Refresh the census; preserve unbound locals, empty cells and rebinding.

**Discard `--assume-plain-attributes` as an implementation recommendation.**
Attributes can run descriptors, raise or see a value changed by an earlier call.
Calls, subscripts and operators have ordinary effects too. The preservation
requirement rules out a flag whose purpose is to bypass these obligations.
Preserve the original paragraph only as historical context.

## Larger retained designs

**Project's own check, P2.** Current `mypy_ci_policy.py` reads literal workflow
strict policy, keeps provenance and refuses conflicting/unsupported syntax. It
does not execute CI or reproduce arbitrary environments. Broad discovery must
model version/platform factors and precedence, reject ambiguous expansion and
report its exact evidence. An explicit structured checker invocation is a smaller
first step. Baseline and prospective checks must use the same policy and still
verify every moved statement and required consumer. Narrow CI targets must not
silently leave changed code unchecked; switching checker or excluding a newly
failing consumer cannot create a green result. Embedded pytest typing and `ty`
remain separate integrations.

**Shared helper module, P2.** Refresh the largest useful library examples; the
old 19,914 host-choice count is not final acceptance. Require a regular common
package and intermediate packages, an installed/entry-point-valid spelling and
an inert leaf host. Model initializer-in-progress cases rather than exempting
import checks broadly. Preserve caller module lookups through inputs/thunks,
body-local imports, annotations, builtin context, collisions and future imports.
Verify actual shipping; explicit build lists and exclusions defeat directory
discovery assumptions. Keep test helpers out of shipped library modules. Growth
and reuse need provenance and per-site binding validation, not a private name.
Publish new files and callers transactionally. Wheel builds execute the project's
backend: retain an explicit trusted-code boundary and check the exact new file.
An opt-in flag is not evidence of distribution inclusion.

**Template Method, P3 research retained.** Read the named refactoring literature,
collect current visitor candidates and hand-judged alternatives, and preserve
visiting-order and hook differences. Existing `OwnScopeVisitor`,
`DefinitionDepthVisitor` and `ScopeVisitor` illustrate intentional design, not
automatic hierarchy correctness. A detector can report without editing bases;
the paired agent may choose an ordinary tested/typed edit. Mechanical new-base,
hook-dispatch or MRO rendering remains ruled out by the September 22 decision.
October 2's reflection exclusion does not revoke that independent design rule.

**User-function reuse and recursion re-rolling.** Keep public-function reuse
withdrawn: ordinary late rebinding couples previously independent callers.
Same-run generated helpers are a different supported case. Retain recursion
re-rolling only as P3 research; syntactic instantiation alone does not establish
callee binding, recursion/exception behavior or lifetime equivalence. It must
not silently restore user-function redirection. Require fresh motivating
examples and a complete preservation argument before adding a pass.

## Recommended order

1. Finish current reproduced audit repairs and their relevant gates before new
   capability or performance work.
2. Refresh a bounded real-input decline census; select one useful recovery.
   Nested liveness and position-specific unification are the strongest retained
   candidates, but their old counts do not settle today's priority.
3. Profile a large current default typed/formatted fixed point with repeated-input
   accounting. Preserve candidate, acceptance and output parity for any exact
   optimization.
4. Define the explicit checker-policy boundary before general CI discovery, and
   package/shipping/import boundaries before new helper-module generation.
5. Keep visitor research and preview formats available without turning them into
   mechanical architecture changes or release blockers.
